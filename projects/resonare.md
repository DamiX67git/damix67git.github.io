## About the project

Resonare: Ancient Echoes explores the theme of echoes from the past.

Its creative direction focuses on forgotten knowledge, mysterious structures and the traces left behind by earlier events.

## Design direction

The aim is to make the world feel like an archive of things that once happened, waiting to be discovered.
